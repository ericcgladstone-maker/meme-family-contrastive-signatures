# Figure 1: source cases and selection procedure

The panels are schematic redraws. No image pixels are reproduced; each panel draws the shared layout of
the development-corpus variants listed in `figure1_sources.csv` (DEV variant IDs + SHA-256 of the local images).

**Actual selection procedure (author-chosen pedagogical cases; no fixed rule is claimed):**
- Candidate variants were taken from each family's top member exemplars in the submitted signature export
  (`analysis/figure_inputs/out/signatures_submitted_export.json`) and viewed by Claude on 2026-09-30.
- (a) *nobody / literally no one* was chosen because its members visibly share a caption formula over unrelated
  base images (phrasal persistence).
- (b) *drakeposting* was chosen because its documented members include the reject/approve two-row layout with
  two different base characters (relational-layout persistence across base templates).
- (c) the look-alike pair uses soyjak/wojak and its highest-scoring nonmember under that family's signature,
  a Pepe the Frog variant (drawn green faces from different documented families). Among the five highest
  top-nonmember fits in the export, this pair was chosen as the most legible schematic.
- No held-out-family image was consulted or used. **Pending Eric's visual approval.**
